Run a blind-spot audit against my diffusion-LM information diet, then record it.

1. Use the `blindspot-scout` subagent to run all three moves: citation /
   backlink expansion from an anchor paper (default D3PM / SEDD / MDLM, or use
   `$ARGUMENTS` if given), cross-domain keyword drift, and naming 5 unwatched
   venues / labs / communities each with one verified recent result. Rank
   findings by how likely they are to change how I'd approach my own work.
2. Append a dated section to the `## Blind-spot audits (monthly)` section of
   `~/diffusion-tracker/diffusion-llm-tracker.md` with the ranked findings and
   their links/tiers.
3. Promote the 2–3 highest-leverage findings into the `## Watchlist`
   (people/labs to track, papers to read) so they don't get lost.

Keep the record skimmable. Preserve every link. Print the ranked findings and
note anything that couldn't be verified.
